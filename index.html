About Me

Hello! I'm a current medical student at the Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai, and I'm passionate about AI applications in medicine. I previously obtained my ScM in Data Science ('25) and my ScB in Applied Mathematics & Biology ('24) from Brown University. I conducted my undergraduate honors thesis through the Badre Lab, where I used machine learning to analyze fMRI scans. I also conducted public health research through the SAHAR Group at NYU, exploring health and nutrition habits among Asian Americans.
